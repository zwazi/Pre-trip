CMV PRE-TRIP INSPECTION SCRIPT

Non-Modernized CDL Vehicle Inspection · Memorization-focused version using repeatable inspection acronyms · Core rhythm: PMS • CBB • WTF • ABC • NL
HOW TO USE THIS SCRIPT
The bracketed acronyms are memory prompts. During the exam, say the full phrase aloud. Example: [PMS + CBB] = “Properly mounted and secure; not cracked, bent, or broken.”
Core rhythm: PMS • CBB • WTF • ABC • NL

UNIVERSAL PATTERNS

Hard metal part [PMS + CBB]

Properly mounted and secure. Not cracked, bent, or broken.

Hose / rubber line [PMS + ABC + NL]

Properly mounted and secure. No abrasions, bulges, or cuts, and not leaking.

Belt [PMS + WTF + 3/4]

Properly mounted and secure. Not worn, torn, or frayed, with no more than three-quarters inch of play.

Fluid component [PMS + CBB + NL]

Properly mounted and secure. Not cracked, bent, or broken, and not leaking. Fluid level is within the safe operating range.

Tire [ABC + PI + FF + TREAD]

No abrasions, bulges, or cuts. Properly inflated, checked with a tire-pressure gauge. Free of foreign objects. Check correct tread depth.

Rim [CBB + NDW]

Not cracked, bent, or broken, with no damage or illegal welds.

Lug nuts [LM + NR]

None loose or missing; no rust trails or shiny threads indicating looseness.

Brake friction surface [PMS + CBB + DRY]

Properly mounted and secure, not cracked or broken, and free of oil or grease.
